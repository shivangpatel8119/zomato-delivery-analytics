# Insights & Recommendations

## Overall KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 38,964 |
| Average Delivery Time | 27 minutes |
| Average Distance | 9.77 km |
| Average Rating | 4.63 / 5 |

## Delivery Performance

Normal: 15.4K orders; Fast: 11.6K; Slow: 8.4K; Very Slow: 3.6K.

Normal and Fast are the largest segments. Slow and Very Slow together account for approximately 30.8% of orders.

**Recommendation:** Monitor Slow and Very Slow deliveries separately to identify operational bottlenecks and improve consistency.

## Peak vs Non-Peak

Peak average delivery time is 31 minutes versus 25 minutes during non-peak periods.

**Recommendation:** Use demand-aware rider allocation, dispatch planning, and peak-period ETA monitoring.

## Traffic

Average delivery time rises from approximately 21–22 minutes under Low traffic to 31–32 minutes during traffic Jams.

**Recommendation:** Incorporate traffic conditions into dispatch, routing, and ETA decisions.

## Weather

Sunny: 22 minutes; Windy: 26; Stormy: 26; Sandstorms: 26; Fog: 29; Cloudy: 29.

**Recommendation:** Use weather-aware operational monitoring and adjust ETA expectations during adverse conditions.

## City Performance

Urban: 23 minutes; Metropolitan: 27; Semi-Urban: 50.

**Recommendation:** Investigate route distance, traffic, restaurant density, rider availability, and geographic coverage in Semi-Urban areas.

## Vehicle Type

Scooter: 25 minutes; Electric Scooter: 25; Motorcycle: 28.

**Recommendation:** Evaluate vehicle allocation alongside distance, traffic, city, and delivery conditions rather than in isolation.

## Customer Rating

Average delivery-person rating is approximately 4.63 / 5.

**Recommendation:** Track delivery performance and ratings together to monitor customer experience.

## Business Recommendations Summary

1. Improve peak-hour rider allocation and dispatch planning.
2. Use traffic-aware ETA and routing decisions.
3. Monitor weather-sensitive delivery conditions.
4. Investigate Semi-Urban delivery bottlenecks.
5. Evaluate vehicle allocation by operational context.
6. Track Slow and Very Slow deliveries as priority segments.
7. Combine delivery performance and customer-rating analysis.

## Analytical Limitation

These findings describe relationships observed in the dataset and do not by themselves prove causation. For example, higher Semi-Urban delivery time may reflect distance, traffic, restaurant availability, rider availability, or other factors.
