# Business Questions

This project analyzes Zomato delivery data to understand the operational factors associated with delivery performance, efficiency, and customer experience.

## Delivery Performance
1. What is the overall average delivery time?
2. What percentage of orders fall into Fast, Normal, Slow, and Very Slow delivery categories?
3. Which delivery conditions are associated with longer delivery times?
4. What are the minimum and maximum observed delivery times?

## Traffic & Peak Operations
5. How does road traffic density affect average delivery time?
6. How does peak-hour demand affect delivery time compared with non-peak periods?
7. How do traffic conditions and peak periods interact?
8. Which combinations of traffic and distance create higher delivery times?

## Distance & Route Efficiency
9. How does delivery distance relate to delivery time?
10. How does average delivery time vary across route-distance buckets?
11. Which high-distance orders also have high delivery times?

## Vehicle & Delivery Operations
12. Does vehicle type influence average delivery time?
13. Does vehicle condition have an observable relationship with delivery performance?
14. How do multiple deliveries affect average delivery time and delivery performance?

## Weather & External Conditions
15. How do different weather conditions affect delivery time?
16. Which weather and traffic combinations are associated with longer deliveries?
17. Does festival status influence delivery time?

## Location & Customer Experience
18. How does delivery performance vary across cities?
19. How does delivery time differ across city categories?
20. How does delivery-person rating relate to delivery time?

## Advanced Analysis
21. Which are the longest delivery orders?
22. Which orders have the longest delivery distances?
23. What are the highest-risk combinations of traffic, weather, and peak-hour conditions?
24. What are the top three longest deliveries within each city?
